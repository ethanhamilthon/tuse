## Why

Provide maintained, optional Lua plugins for common setups without bundling them into the binary.

## What Changes

- Publish `login`, `themes`, `status`, and `rename` in the repository.
- Let the interactive download installer offer the plugins individually after installing Tuse.
- Document the official catalog and the current namespaced configuration.

## Impact

`plugins`, `examples`, release installer, and usage documentation. Noninteractive installs and existing configs are unchanged.
